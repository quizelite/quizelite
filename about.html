About QuizElite

Free, simple and fun computer hardware quizzes — no sign-up, no downloads.

QuizElite was created to help students, builders and tech enthusiasts test and improve their knowledge of PC hardware: CPUs, RAM, storage, GPUs, motherboards, power supplies and peripherals.


Why use QuizElite?

  • 100% free quizzes with instant scoring
  • Multiple categories and difficulty levels
  • Questions written to reflect real-world hardware concepts
  • New questions added regularly
